!
Bottleneck signals describe documented themes.

Ten coded signal fields cover validation, reproducibility, performance, applicability, mechanistic relevance, product matrices, regulatory integration, prioritisation, access and standardisation. Multiple signals may apply to a method; they do not by themselves establish why a regulatory outcome occurred.

Limitations

Read patterns with care

  • TSAR is a tracking system, not a complete literature or evidence database.
  • Absence from TSAR does not mean a method is nonexistent, irrelevant or unused.
  • Validation, recommendation, standardisation, acceptance and implementation are related but not interchangeable.
  • Observed differences are descriptive and exploratory; the website does not make causal or predictive claims.
