Traceable by design

From source record to analysis-ready data

The pipeline keeps original TSAR fields separate from derived classifications, manual review and exploratory screening signals.

01

TSAR source

Public method descriptions, stages, topics and organisations remain identifiable.

02

Derived fields

Documented rules create endpoint, methodology, regulatory and 3R classifications.

03

Verification

Core and case-study verification fields record researcher verification for all 147 methods in this release.

04

Analysis

Binary indicators support counts; readable labels support interpretation and checking.

!
Bottleneck signals describe documented themes.

Ten coded signal fields cover validation, reproducibility, performance, applicability, mechanistic relevance, product matrices, regulatory integration, prioritisation, access and standardisation. Multiple signals may apply to a method; they do not by themselves establish why a regulatory outcome occurred.

Know what came from TSAR and what was derived

TSAR source identifies imported fields. Derived identifies classifications or helper variables created for this research.

Regulatory pathway, endpoint, methodology, 3R and verification fields are separated from the source layer. AI-assisted classifications were used within a structured review workflow and were subsequently researcher verified where applicable.

Limitations

Read patterns with care

  • TSAR is a tracking system, not a complete literature or evidence database.
  • Absence from TSAR does not mean a method is nonexistent, irrelevant or unused.
  • Validation, recommendation, standardisation, acceptance and implementation are related but not interchangeable.
  • Observed differences are descriptive and exploratory; the website does not make causal or predictive claims.
