Research release · 25 September 2026

From TSAR records to a research-ready regulatory landscape

This research project turns a snapshot of 147 test-method records from the European Commission Joint Research Centre’s Tracking System for Alternative Methods towards Regulatory Acceptance (TSAR) into a transparent, quality-controlled dataset. It examines which New Approach Methodologies (NAMs) progress, where pathways slow or stop, and how endpoints and methods differ across regulatory outcomes.

Conceptual framework

The pathway towards regulatory acceptance

A conceptual progression from initial scientific innovation to formal regulatory uptake.

1Step 1

Method development

Scientific design, protocol optimisation and applicability domain definition.

2Step 2

Validation

Formal assessment of reliability, reproducibility and toxicological relevance.

3Step 3

Peer review

Independent scientific scrutiny and validation body review opinions.

4Step 4

Regulatory evaluation

Appraisal by regulatory authorities for specific decision-making contexts.

5Step 5

Guideline / regulatory acceptance

Formal inclusion in OECD Test Guidelines or jurisdictional regulatory standards.

6Step 6

Regulatory use

Routine deployment in risk assessments, classification and dossier filings.

Important caveat: Not every method follows every step, and regulatory pathways may differ between methods and jurisdictions.
